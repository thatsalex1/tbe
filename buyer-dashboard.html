❔ Frequently Asked Questions

How long does verification take?

Account verification is instant. Deposit confirmation takes up to 24 hours depending on the payment method.

What if the seller doesn't ship?

Our support team will investigate. If the seller fails to fulfill their obligation, we'll refund your escrow amount fully.

Are there any transaction fees?

We charge a small escrow fee (1-2%) only when transactions complete successfully. No hidden charges.

Can I cancel a transaction?

Yes, before the seller ships. After shipment confirmation, cancellation requires seller agreement.
