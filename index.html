How Escrow Works

How Escrow Works

Step 1
Agreement
Both parties agree on the deal terms — coin, amount, and what's being exchanged.
Step 2
Fund Escrow
The buyer sends crypto to a dedicated escrow address and we confirm it on-chain.
Step 3
Deliver
The seller delivers the agreed goods, service, or asset.
Step 4
Release Crypto
Once the buyer confirms receipt, the crypto is released to the seller's wallet instantly.
